# 🌍 Tech Naija Rise (TNR)

**Building Software for Nigeria, by Nigeria.**

---

## 👋 About Us

Tech Naija Rise (TNR) is a software company focused on solving problems that actually matter for Nigerians.
We create:

* **Offline-first tools** that don’t break when the internet does.
* **Lightweight apps** that run smoothly on a wide range of devices.
* **Secure, interconnected solutions** designed to be fast and reliable.

---

## 🛠️ What We’re Working On

Our flagship project is:

**BM (BobsiMo)** – a family of productivity tools that are:

* Fast, simple, and reliable.
* Built to work offline.
* Designed for students, programmers, and everyday users.

---

## 🎯 Our North Star

> “Build a suite of lightweight, secure, and interconnected tools that are fully offline-friendly, fast, and reliable—designed specifically to work smoothly in Nigeria’s real-world conditions.”
